# Flight Team preview

Open **http://127.0.0.1:8090/?demo=1** to explore the sample workspace.

If the preview server has stopped, start it from this folder:

```powershell
python -m http.server 8090 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory docs
```

- Click the four summary cards to see the corresponding products.
- Open a product to explore assignment dropdowns and the promotion expiration date.
- Open Completed to see a sample expired promotion.
- Open Team > Add teammate to preview the administrator form (saving is disabled in the demo).
- Open Brand Contacts, then click a brand card to view contact details and notes.
- Open Marketing notes to explore a sample meeting and action items.
- Switch your system appearance to see light and dark themes.

The sample uses fictional data and disables saving. The connected app continues to use your existing Supabase records. Run the unapplied migrations through `supabase/06-brand-contacts.sql` in Supabase to enable contacts, meeting notes, promotion dates, and administrator-managed teammates, then publish the updated `docs/` assets using your existing deployment workflow.
